# Synthetic notebook (ground truth)

Invented entries for testing and the demo. Write them **by hand** on two pages of a ruled notebook, so Giveback reads real handwriting while every fact stays made up. Copy each line the way it's written below, mixing Telugu and English as shown. Don't tidy it up: crossings-out and uneven spacing are good.

The notebook belongs to the fictional **Bandaru family** (Subba Rao and Lakshmi). Lakshmi's maiden name is Butham, so her brother Muthyalu is "Muthyalu mama" to the children, and his wife Vimala is "Vimala atha".

Tip: use a blue ballpoint, take the photo in daylight, and keep the phone a little tilted on page 2 so the test isn't too easy.

## Traps built in on purpose

- **Same person, different spellings:** row 1.1 "ముత్యాలు మామ" (relation word) and row 2.1 "భూతం ముత్యాలు" (full name) are the same person. The card says "Sri Butham Muthyalu".
- **Same first name, different person:** "Muthyalu, Chityal" must not be merged with Butham Muthyalu.
- **Same surname, different person:** "B. Narsimha" shares the initial B. and the town Nalgonda.
- **116 vs 1,116:** rows 1.2, 1.5 and 2.1.
- **The other side of the card:** Puram Nagaiah is named on the card as the bride's father, not as the host.
- **Gifts with no cash:** gold in sovereigns (కాసు), a silver plate, a saree.
