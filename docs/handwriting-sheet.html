Notebook copy sheet

Copy each entry onto one line of a ruled notebook, in blue ballpoint. Big letters here only so every Telugu mark is clear. Write at your normal size.

  • Each entry goes on its own line. Leave a little space between the name, the dash, and the amount.
  • Keep Telugu and English exactly as shown. Don't translate a line.
  • Copy the amounts exactly: 116 and 1116 are different, and only page 2 has the comma in 1,116.
  • Four entries have no amount (a ring, a silver plate, a saree, a chain). Don't add one.
  • Unsure of a Telugu letter? See the letter guide below the pages.

Telugu letter guide

Every Telugu word on the two pages, split into the letters you write one after another. A grey box is one letter with its marks. A box with a letter written under another (like త్త) is a joined pair: write the first letter, then the small second letter below it.

పళ్లెం and పెళ్ళి use two different ways of writing the double ḷ. Both are correct; copy each as shown.
